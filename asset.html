1 · Price with two moving averages

▲ green = fast average crosses above slow (possible buy), ▼ red = crosses below (possible sell). Dashed orange lines = your price alerts.

2 · RSI

Above 70: price rose fast and may be stretched. Below 30: it fell fast. Scale 0–100.

3 · MACD (12 / 26 / 9 hours)

MACD line crossing above its signal line = momentum turning up; below = turning down. Bars = the gap between them.

4 · Bollinger Bands

The band widens when the price swings a lot. Price near the upper band = stretched up; near the lower = stretched down.

5 · Backtest on your saved history

Starts with 100 at the beginning of the selected period. Long only (in the asset or in cash). Orange = the rule, grey = just holding the asset.
A backtest shows what would have happened. It does not predict the future, and trying many settings until the result looks good gives results that will not repeat.
